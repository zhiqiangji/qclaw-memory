## ✅ Step 1：Manuscript Type & Core Message

- **Type**: Full research article (rock mechanics, experimental + theoretical)
- **Core topic**: Elastoplastic response of bedded sandstone with bedding anisotropy under freeze-thaw (F-T) cycles
- **Key findings**:
  1. Apparent elastic modulus has **two-stage evolution** with plastic strain: ↑ (compaction/pore closure) → ↓ (yielding/crack propagation); apparent Poisson's ratio increases continuously
  2. F-T cycling acts as an **"anisotropy amplifier"**: most significantly reduces stiffness at intermediate bedding orientations (β ≈ 45°)
  3. Established quantitative relationships, derived evolution equations for 5 independent elastic constants of transversely isotropic model, formed a complete constitutive framework

- **Contribution**: Fills gap in understanding how F-T cycles interact with bedding anisotropy on elastoplastic evolution
