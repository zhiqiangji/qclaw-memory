摘   要
头道沟一号隧道作为河北某高速公路的关键工程，其左幅隧道穿越Ⅲ~Ⅴ级围岩，存在超浅埋、浅埋、深埋三种状态。本设计以头道沟一号隧道（右幅）工程为背景，进行隧道的结构设计。隧道全长1060 m，设计行车速度80 km/h，采用单洞双车道的断面形式。结构设计：采用复合式衬砌（初期支护+二次衬砌），通过MIDAS GTS NX软件验证二次衬砌的安全性。针对防水，进行防排水设计。
关键词：隧道；设计；二次衬砌
ABSTRACT
Toudaogou Tunnel No.1, as a key engineering project of a certain highway, features its left tunnel crossing through III to V grade rock masses with three geological conditions: super-shallow burial, shallow burial, and deep burial. This design focuses on the structural design of the right tunnel of Toudaogou Tunnel No.1, with a total length of 1060 meters, a design speed of 80 km/h, and a single-bored dual-lane cross-section. The structural design employs composite lining （primary support + secondary lining）, and the safety of the secondary lining is verified using MIDAS GTS NX software. Additionally, drainage and waterproofing designs are implemented for water management.
Key words: Tunnel; Design; Secondary lining
