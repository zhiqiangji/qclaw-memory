摘  要
本文以河北省CQ高速公路XS分离式隧道右幅工程为研究对象，对该项目全长750m（桩号范围YK8+700-YK9+450）的紫红色砾岩和细砂岩组成Ⅲ、Ⅳ、Ⅴ级围岩段进行分析。按照公路工程技术标准中高速公路Ⅰ级公路的技术指标进行设计，设计速度为100 km/h，在满足设计要求的基础上，根据规范进行初步设计工作，确定相应参数以及计算方式，进行设计和验算。根据施工辅助措施的需要来制定施工方案，对于防排水、地质预报等进行设计。根据不同的围岩类别，经由科学的工程类比分析及专项计算来验证，从而产生出具备较强适应性的复合式衬砌设计。最后形成完整的报告和施工图既符合国家有关标准和技术规范的要求，也达到了本科毕业设计综合训练教学目标的需要。
关键词：隧道；复合式衬砌；围岩分级；通风照明设计
ABSTRACT
This paper takes the right tube of XS separated tunnel of CQ Expressway in Hebei Province as the research subject. The studied section spans 750 meters from stake YK8+700 to YK9+450, consisting of purplish-red conglomerate and fine sandstone with Grade III, IV and V surrounding rocks.The design complies with technical criteria for Class I expressways with a design speed of 100 km/h. Preliminary design is implemented per relevant specifications to determine design parameters and calculation methods, followed by structural design and verification. Construction schemes are formulated matching auxiliary construction requirements, along with designs for waterproofing, drainage and geological prediction.Adopting engineering analogy and specialized calculation based on varying surrounding rock grades, adaptive composite lining structure is established. The finalized design documents and construction drawings conform to national codes and specifications, fulfilling the comprehensive training objectives of undergraduate graduation design.
Key words:Tunnel;  Composite lining;  Surrounding rock classification;  Ventilation and lighting design
