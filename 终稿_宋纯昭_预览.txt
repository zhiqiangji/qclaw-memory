摘   要
CQ高速公路TDG-1隧道设计（左幅）为背景为某高速公路关键控制性工程，隧道全长1080 m，设计行车速度 120 km/h，采用单洞三车道断面形式。隧址区地质条件复杂，穿越 Ⅲ～Ⅴ 级围岩，存在超浅埋、浅埋、深埋三种结构受力状态。设计严格遵循现行公路隧道规范，采用复合式衬砌结构形式，由初期支护与二次衬砌共同承担围岩荷载；运用 MIDAS GTS NX 有限元软件建立数值模型，完成二次衬砌内力分析与安全验算，确保结构长期稳定可靠；同时结合工程水文条件，开展完善的隧道防排水系统设计，保障隧道运营安全与耐久性。本设计综合考虑地质条件、结构安全与施工可行性，形成一套完整、经济、适用的隧道结构设计方案。
关键词：隧道；结构设计；二次衬砌
ABSTRACT
The CQ Expressway TDG-1 Tunnel Design (Left Lane), serving as a critical control project on the expressway, spans 1,080 meters with a design speed of 120 km/h and features a single-bore three-lane cross-section. The tunnel site exhibits complex geological conditions, traversing Grade III to V surrounding rock formations and presenting three structural stress states: ultra-shallow burial, shallow burial, and deep burial. The design strictly adheres to current highway tunnel codes, employing a composite lining system where primary support and secondary lining jointly bear the surrounding rock loads. A numerical model was developed using MIDAS GTS NX finite element software to conduct internal force analysis and safety verification of the secondary lining, ensuring long-term structural stability and reliability. Additionally, a comprehensive tunnel drainage and waterproofing system was designed considering hydrological conditions to guarantee operational safety and durability. This design integrates geological considerations, structural safety, and construction feasibility, resulting in a complete, cost-effective, and practical tunnel structure solution.
Key words； Tunnel； Design； Secondary lining
